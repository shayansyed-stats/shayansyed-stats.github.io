Shayan Syed

Shayan Syed is a PhD student in Psychology at Lakehead University. His work examines moral values, political identity, social affiliation, and artificial intelligence in education and research.

He is also a university instructor, research-methods educator, track and field coach, and community volunteer. This website shares his research, teaching resources, projects, and professional work.
