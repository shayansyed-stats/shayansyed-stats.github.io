About Shayan Syed

Shayan Syed is a PhD student in Psychology at Lakehead University in Thunder Bay, Ontario. His academic work focuses on moral psychology, political identity, social affiliation, research methods, and the responsible use of artificial intelligence in education and research.

Research and teaching

Shayan's research examines how people use political labels and moral values when making decisions about potential social partners. He is interested in research design, replication, quantitative analysis, and producing clear, practical research resources.

Alongside his graduate research, Shayan has supported psychology teaching across introductory and advanced courses. His teaching interests include social psychology, cognitive psychology, research methods, psychometrics, academic learning, and student-facing guidance.

Technology, coaching, and community

Shayan explores practical and privacy-conscious uses of local artificial intelligence tools for research and education. He also coaches track and field and has contributed to community-based initiatives in Thunder Bay.
