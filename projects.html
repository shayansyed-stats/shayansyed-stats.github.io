Public work and resources

Research, Teaching, and AI Projects

Shayan Syed develops research, teaching, and technology projects related to psychology, quantitative methods, student learning, and responsible artificial intelligence. This page provides an overview of current and developing work, with public resources added when they are suitable to share.

Current areas of project work

Research methods

Reproducible psychology research workflows

Practical approaches to organizing survey data, documenting analysis decisions, evaluating data quality, and supporting transparent, reproducible research in psychology.

Data analysis

Survey-data cleaning and screening

Resources and workflows for preparing survey datasets, checking variable coding, identifying missing or implausible values, reviewing response quality, and documenting decisions before analysis.

AI in education

AI literacy and responsible use

Educational material on using artificial intelligence critically and responsibly in learning, writing, research, and information evaluation, with attention to academic integrity and privacy.

Local tools

Privacy-conscious research technology

Exploration of local and open-source language-model tools for research and education, including document question-answering and workflows that limit unnecessary sharing of sensitive information.

Teaching

Student-facing psychology resources

Clear, practical guides intended to help students understand research methods, evaluate evidence, manage academic work, and develop stronger research literacy.

Research communication

Public-facing research explanations

Plain-language explanations of work in moral psychology, political psychology, social affiliation, and the relationship between psychological research and everyday social decisions.

How these projects connect

These projects reflect a shared interest in making psychological research more understandable, rigorous, and practically useful. They connect Shayan's work in moral and political psychology research with his psychology teaching and student resources.

New public resources will be added as they are completed or prepared for sharing. Materials are designed to be accurate, useful, and appropriate for public access rather than duplicating restricted course content or unpublished research materials.
