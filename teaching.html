Teaching and learning

Psychology Teaching and Student Resources

Shayan Syed supports psychology teaching and student learning at Lakehead University in Thunder Bay, Ontario. His teaching work emphasizes clear communication, thoughtful learning practices, research literacy, and practical skills that students can apply in academic and professional settings.

Teaching areas

Introduction to psychology

Core psychological concepts, research literacy, scientific reasoning, and connections between psychology and everyday life.

Social psychology

Social judgment, identity, attitudes, morality, relationships, cooperation, group processes, and political behaviour.

Research methods

Study design, hypothesis testing, measurement, survey research, data quality, ethics, interpretation, and replication.

Tests and measurement

Psychometrics, reliability, validity, scale construction, assessment, and interpreting psychological measures.

Cognition and learning

Memory, attention, reasoning, learning strategies, and the role of cognitive tools in educational settings.

AI literacy

Responsible and critical use of artificial intelligence in learning, academic writing, research, and information evaluation.

Approach to teaching

Effective psychology teaching should make complex ideas understandable without oversimplifying them. Shayan's approach combines clear explanations, structured examples, and opportunities for students to connect concepts to research questions, real-world decisions, and their own learning.

He is particularly interested in helping students develop research literacy: the ability to ask good questions, evaluate evidence, understand methods, interpret results cautiously, and communicate conclusions responsibly.
